What Flowers to Give Your Girlfriend

Choosing flowers for a girl depends on the occasion and her personal preferences. For a first date, soft and romantic flowers are a great choice.

Best Flowers for a First Date

  • Tulips: symbolize happiness and create a light, cheerful mood.
  • Roses: pink and pastel roses express affection and tenderness.
  • Peonies: romantic flowers that are perfect for someone special.
  • Eustomas: soft and elegant flowers that make a bouquet look delicate.
  • Daisies: simple and cheerful flowers, perfect for a sweet romantic gift.

What Do Flower Colors Mean?

  • Red: love and passion
  • Pink: tenderness and affection
  • White: purity and sincerity
  • Yellow: happiness and friendship

Our Tip

The best bouquet is the one that matches her personality and preferences. If you are not sure what to choose, go for soft pink or white flowers — they are a beautiful choice for almost any occasion.

What Flowers to Give Your Mom

Flowers are a beautiful way to show your mom love, gratitude, and care. When choosing a bouquet, think about her favorite colors, flowers, and personality.

Best Flowers for Mom

  • Roses: symbolize love and respect.
  • Peonies: represent happiness, warmth, and comfort.
  • Tulips: a fresh and cheerful choice, especially in spring.
  • Chrysanthemums: symbolize happiness and long life.
  • Orchids: elegant flowers for moms who love something special.

Choose by Season

  • Spring: tulips and hyacinths.
  • Summer: peonies and daisies.
  • Autumn: chrysanthemums and asters.
  • Winter: roses, orchids, and eustomas.

Our Tip

Choose flowers that match your mom's personality. Bright colors are great for energetic moms, while soft pastel colors are perfect for calm and romantic personalities.

If your mom is sensitive to strong scents, avoid highly fragrant flowers such as lilies. Add a small card with warm words to make your gift more personal.

How to Keep Flowers Fresh

Fresh flowers can stay beautiful longer with proper care. Follow these simple tips to keep your bouquet fresh.

Simple Flower Care Tips

  1. Use clean water: Fill a clean vase with fresh, cool water.
  2. Trim the stems: Cut about 1–2 cm from the stems at an angle before placing the flowers in water.
  3. Change the water: Replace the water every 1–2 days to keep it fresh and clean.
  4. Remove extra leaves: Remove any leaves that are below the water level. This helps keep the water clean.
  5. Keep flowers in a cool place: Avoid direct sunlight, heaters, and other hot places.

Our Tip

Check your flowers every day, remove wilted flowers, and keep the vase clean. With a little care, your bouquet can stay fresh and beautiful for longer.
